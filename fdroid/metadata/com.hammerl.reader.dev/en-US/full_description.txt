Development channel of Mediamaster. This build updates on every CI pipeline and may be unstable — install the stable "Mediamaster" app for everyday use. Both can be installed side by side.

Mediamaster is a unified client for your self-hosted media library:

Books: read EPUBs, PDFs and comics served by Calibre-Web, with offline downloads and reading-progress sync.

Video: stream movies and TV shows from Jellyfin — resume, audio-track switching and subtitles.

Music: browse and play your Jellyfin music library with queues, shuffle and repeat.

Audiobooks & podcasts: listen through Audiobookshelf with progress synced across devices.

One app, one set of credentials (LDAP/AD) for everything, with offline support.
